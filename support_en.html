FAQ

  • Does it need the internet? No. Only purchases need an App Store connection.
  • Will I lose data if I change devices? Progress is saved on the device and in your own iCloud (no game server). Signed in with the same Apple Account, a reinstall or a new device continues automatically on first launch. Without iCloud, deleting the App removes the progress.
  • I paid but did not receive my items. Quit and relaunch the App; pending purchases are delivered automatically. If not, contact us with your Apple receipt.
  • What does "Restore Purchases" restore? Lasting rights only: the Rookie Pack and the Starter Pack stay purchased (one each per account, no second payment) and the Champion Pass keeps the rest of its 30 days counted from the original purchase. Tickets and gold (from the Rookie Pack, the Starter Pack, the pass's 5 instant tickets and consumables) are granted once, at purchase; after a reinstall they come back with your progress saved in iCloud, not from purchase history.
  • What is the Rookie Pack? 5 recruit tickets and 2,000 gold, one purchase per account (non-consumable). The tickets and gold are granted once, at purchase. After reinstalling or on a new device, "Restore Purchases" restores only the purchased state (it cannot be bought again); the tickets and gold are not granted again and come back with your progress saved in iCloud. Its tickets are ordinary tickets with the same odds and guarantees as below.
  • What is in the 120 and 250 ticket packs? 120 recruit tickets are 100 plus 20 bonus, 250 recruit tickets are 200 plus 50 bonus (55 recruit tickets are 50 plus 5 bonus); all are consumables. Bonus tickets are ordinary tickets, and buying a pack does not change the odds: the tables below apply as they are. Because an ordinary recruit guarantees an SSS within at most 200 recruits (pity; free and paid recruits both count), using all 250 tickets on ordinary recruits yields at least one SSS under that guarantee. Nothing beyond that guarantee is promised (no extra SSS and no specific fighter); which SSS fighter appears is decided with equal probability.
  • What is in the gold packs? 15,000 gold, 42,000 gold (37,500 plus 4,500 bonus) and 90,000 gold (75,000 plus 15,000 bonus); all are consumables. They are not random items: the amount shown is granted once per purchase. Gold is spent on skills, Gym Sessions, XP Transfer and the ticket exchange (see "What is gold for?"). The ticket exchange is capped at 5 a week, and turning purchased gold into tickets costs more than buying a ticket pack. Unused gold can be withdrawn within 7 days of purchase; an approved refund takes back the gold that purchase granted (the balance may go negative if it was spent). Restore Purchases and reinstalling do not grant it again; it comes back with your progress saved in iCloud.
  • Where do I get recruit tickets? Free, by playing: 10 tickets when you start; 1 ticket for the first win over each ranked contender (up to 7 per tournament) and 1 for the first win of each tournament's title fight (winning a tournament can also give a reward-only ticket or a fighter selector, depending on the tournament); tickets on set days of the check-in calendar (each day's reward is shown on the calendar); 1 ticket for finishing all 3 daily missions; 1 ticket the first time each day you reach the Daily Challenge Gold tier (9,000); Weekly Rank Season rewards by the week's highest tier (L4–L6 1 · L7 and above 2); 1 ticket for first clearing the Skill School final exam; challenges (Giant Hunt and Last Stand 1 each, The SSS Wall 2, on the first clear); 1 ticket for first beating each rival in a rematch; 1 ticket for beating the special challenger that appears every 5th straight Title Defense win; collection rewards once per grade when you own every fighter of that grade (some grades include reward-only tickets); and exchanges: 3,000 gold per ticket in the shop (up to 5 per week) or 30 medals per ticket in the medal shop. Paid: the Rookie Pack, the Starter Pack, the Champion Pass and the 10, 55, 120 and 250 ticket packs in the shop include tickets.
  • What does check-in give? One check-in reward a day; missing a day never resets it, you simply get the next day's reward. New players get the Welcome Week for their first 7 check-ins: Day 1 1,000 gold · Day 2 2 recruit tickets · Day 3 10 medals · Day 4 2,000 gold · Day 5 3 recruit tickets · Day 6 20 medals · Day 7 an A Pick Voucher (choose any A-grade fighter). The Welcome Week runs once; from the day after its 7th check-in the weekly calendar repeats (300 gold · 500 gold · 1 ticket · 800 gold · 1 ticket · 1,000 gold · 1 ticket and 1 B-or-better ticket). Accounts that played before this version stay on the weekly calendar.
  • When do daily rewards reset? Check-in, daily missions, the Champion Pass reward, Gym Sessions and training rewards reset at midnight, and the gold-to-ticket limit every Monday at midnight, in the time zone you first played in. After travelling, the App switches to the new time zone at a moment when no day is skipped or repeated. Changing the device clock never makes a collected day available again; after setting it back, a new day starts only once real time passes the last time recorded.
  • What is gold for? Learning skills (unlock Lv.10 500 · Lv.20 1,500 · Lv.30 3,500 · Lv.45 7,000 · Lv.60 12,000 gold); Gym Sessions on the fighter screen (XP = 100 + 40 × your highest fighter level, 5 a day across all fighters, priced at 1× · 1.5× · 2× · 3× · 4× the XP in gold for the 1st to 5th session of the day, rounded up to 10, reset at midnight); XP Transfer on the fighter screen (take XP from a higher-level fighter you own: 80% of the XP taken arrives, 20% is lost, 1 gold per 4 XP taken; the receiver cannot pass the giver's current level, the giver drops to the level its remaining XP gives, learned skills stay learned but cannot be used below their unlock level; cannot be undone); and recruit tickets in the shop (3,000 gold each, up to 5 per week starting Monday; they are ordinary tickets with the same odds and guarantees).
  • How do the tournament rankings work? Each of the 7 tournaments has 15 ranked contenders under its champion. You start unranked and first challenge #15; after that you may challenge anyone up to 3 places above you. A win takes that place (the others move down one), a loss drops you one place (never below #15), and you can rematch anyone above you. Reaching #1 unlocks the title fight against the champion; winning it makes you champion and opens the next tournament. If you lose the title fight you keep #1, but you must win one comeback bout against a contender ranked #2–#4 before the rematch (losing that bout drops you one place). The first win over each contender pays the rewards shown on its row; a rematch win pays reduced rewards. There is no difficulty setting: opponents get stronger with the rank and the tournament.
  • What is the Daily Challenge? One special match a day on the Events screen, open after your first tournament win. Everyone gets the same opponent, arena and special rule on the same date (for example Counter ×2, No Guard or One-Down Match); the opponent's level follows your main fighter. You have 3 attempts a day (one is used when the fight starts) and your best score of the day goes to the Game Center daily leaderboard. Score: win 3,000 · KO 1,500 (TKO 1,200) · 15 per second left · clean hit 40 · counter 150 · perfect dodge 100 · knockdown 300 · 3-hit combo 100, minus damage taken (1,000 per full health bar, at most 2,000). Rewards: first win of the day gives gold (150 + 5 × main fighter level, up to 600) and XP (80 + 10 × level); reaching a score tier for the first time that day gives Bronze (3,000) 3 medals, Silver (6,000) 200 gold and 5 medals, Gold (9,000) 1 recruit ticket; a 7-day win streak adds 1,000 gold. Each reward is given once a day and resets at midnight like the other daily rewards (time zone you first played in); the Game Center daily leaderboard follows Apple's day (midnight UTC).
  • What is the Weekly Rank Season? A weekly ranked mode on the Events screen (Rank Season tab), open after your first tournament win; it replaced the Weekly Defense League. Your tournament (campaign) progress, titles and Hall of Fame are never reset; only the season tier climbs again every week. Tiers are the tournaments, from L1 to the last one, and your season tier can never pass the highest tournament you have opened. Each tier has 5 stars: a season win +1 star, a loss −1 star (never below 0), a draw no change; 5 stars promote you to the next tier. Your tier never drops during the week. With a full bar at the highest tournament you have opened, you move up as soon as you open the next one. Opponents are that tier's ranked contenders (around #15, #12, #9, #6 and #3 for 0–4 stars; the last is the promotion bout) with that tournament's level, strength, AI, rounds and arena, and the same handicap rule. Bouts are unlimited and wins give XP (40 + 6 × main fighter level); closing the App mid-fight counts as a loss when you start the next bout. Every Monday at midnight (the time zone you first played in) the week ends: with at least 1 win that week you get the reward of your highest tier once, and the new week starts two tiers below it (L1 at the lowest); a week without bouts counts its starting tier as its highest, so you drop two more. The reward is added automatically at the change (or the next time you open the App) and shown in a results card. Setting the device clock back never reopens a past week. Your first season starts two tiers below the highest tournament you have opened (L1 at the lowest). Weekly rewards by highest tier: L1 300 gold, 2 medals · L2 500 gold, 3 medals · L3 800 gold, 5 medals · L4 1,100 gold, 7 medals, 1 recruit ticket · L5 1,500 gold, 10 medals, 1 ticket · L6 2,000 gold, 13 medals, 1 ticket · L7 2,500 gold, 16 medals, 2 tickets · L8 and above 3,000 gold, 20 medals, 2 tickets. The Game Center weekly leaderboard (Apple's week, Monday midnight UTC) shows your best of the week as tier number × 1,000 + stars × 100 (L4 with 3 stars = 4,300). Any Weekly Defense League tier reward you had not collected is paid once, with the old table, the first time you open the App after the update.
  • What is the Skill School? Nine lessons on the Training screen where you learn the advanced moves hands-on against a sparring partner on the real fight screen (spacing, guard and stamina, slip/weave/sway, dodge counters, counter chains, dash and backstep, corner pivot and roll-through, skill commands, final exam). Lessons open in order and pay once, on the first clear: lessons 1–2 give 100 gold and 60 XP, lessons 3, 4 and 6 give 150 gold and 80 XP, lessons 5, 7 and 8 give 200 gold and 100 XP, and lesson 9 (final exam) gives 300 gold, 150 XP and 1 recruit ticket (1,550 gold and 1 ticket in total). XP goes to your main fighter. Stars (up to 3) are a record only and do not change the reward; replays keep your best. There are no knockdowns or KOs in a lesson.
  • Refunds: Request through reportaproblem.apple.com within 7 days for unused items. When a refund is approved, the currency granted by that purchase is removed once; if already spent, the balance may go negative ("unsettled") and is offset by later earnings. Fighters and progress are kept.
