Boxing: Champion Road Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-21

Effective: December 1, 2026 · 한국어

Dandokhaengdong ("we") respects the privacy of users of "Boxing: Champion Road" (the "App").

1. Data the App processes

  • The App does not collect personal data or send it to us. There is no account, login or game server, and the game works offline.
  • Game progress (fighters, levels, skills, tournament progress, currencies, recruitment history, settings) is stored on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, a copy is kept in your own iCloud (Apple key-value storage) so you can continue after reinstalling or on a new device. We cannot access it.
  • If you use Game Center, achievements and leaderboard scores (best title-defense streak, fighters owned) are sent to Apple Game Center under Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive them.
  • If you allow notifications, reward and mission reminders are scheduled on the device; no server is involved.
  • We do not use ads, analytics, location, contacts, camera/photos, microphone or the advertising identifier (IDFA), and we do not track you.
  • We do not share personal data with third parties, outsource its processing, or transfer it abroad.

2. In-app purchases

Payment information is handled by Apple; we never receive or store it. The App checks only the purchase result and transaction ID on the device to deliver items and to prevent double delivery. Apple's privacy policy applies.

3. Support requests

If you contact us by email or phone, we process your email address or phone number, your message and any attachments (e.g. receipts, screenshots) to answer you and to handle payment disputes. Records are kept for 3 years after resolution as required by Korean consumer-protection law, otherwise deleted without delay. Phone calls go through a privacy-safe relay number (+82 50-6825-8576) and are not recorded.

4. Retention and deletion

App data stays on your device and in your own iCloud. Deleting the App deletes the device copy; "Reset data" in Settings also deletes the iCloud copy. If you use iOS device backups (e.g. iCloud), App data may be included in your own backup according to your Apple account settings.

5. Children

The App collects no personal data from anyone, regardless of age. Requests from children under 14 are handled with the consent of a legal guardian.

6. Your rights

You can request access, correction, deletion or suspension of processing for any support information we hold.
